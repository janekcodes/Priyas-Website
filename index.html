chapter one

where it all began

I still think about the day we met. It was one of our first days at SFU in 2021, sitting in Psych 100, when I found myself sitting beside you. I remember thinking you were beautiful, but I had no idea that the person sitting next to me would become my best friend and such an important part of my life. What started as a simple seat beside someone new slowly turned into countless conversations, endless laughs, and a friendship I’ll always be grateful for. Looking back now, it’s crazy to think that one small moment in a classroom would become the beginning of our story.

chapter two

when everything changed

September 7th, 2023. I still remember this day so clearly. After class, we went up to the top of Burnaby Mountain just to enjoy the view and spend a little more time together. You were wearing a black t-shirt and blue jeans, and somehow we ended up matching without even planning it. I remember looking at you with the wind blowing through your hair, seeing how relaxed and happy you were, and thinking about how beautiful you were. Not just because of how you looked, but because of how you made me feel. Being with you felt peaceful. It felt easy. It felt like I could just be myself. Standing there with you, looking out at the view, I realized there was no one I would rather spend my time with. You had become my favourite person, the person I wanted to laugh with, talk to, and make memories with. I didn’t know it in that exact moment, but that day became one of the most important pages in our story.

chapter three

the little things i love about you

I could probably write an entire book about all the things I love about you. I love the way you laugh at your own jokes before you even finish telling them. I love how you can make any ordinary moment feel special just by being there. I love the way you care about the people around you, the way you make me feel comfortable being myself, and the way you always make even the simplest moments feel like memories I want to keep forever. But more than anything, I love the person you are. You are kind, beautiful, funny, smart, and someone who makes the world feel a little brighter. Every memory with you is one I’m grateful for, and I hope you always know how much you mean to me.

chapter four

the story we're still writing

And somehow, after everything, here we are. What started as two people sitting beside each other in a classroom turned into years of memories, laughter, and a friendship that became something so much more. When I look back at everything we've shared, I realize how many little moments became some of my favourite memories. But the best part is that this isn't the end of our story. It's just another page. There are still so many laughs to have, places to see, memories to make, and moments waiting for us. This is only the beginning of the story we're still writing. 🩵
